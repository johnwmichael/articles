# Chapter 1 — Kreuzberg, West Berlin

**Status:** Draft — in interview. Facts below are documented; recollection sections are being gathered from John.
**Time period:** Late 1970s – early 1980s (approx.)

---

## What We Know (documented)

- John grew up in **Kreuzberg, West Berlin**, during the Cold War era — the city was divided, and West Berlin was an enclave inside East Germany.
- He was **fluent in German** as a child; the language faded after years in the United States. He is now deliberately reclaiming it.
- His best friend there was **Bernhard** — still missed decades later.
- He **misses the bread** 🍞 (a recurring, affectionate theme in our conversations).
- He watched *Dogs of Berlin*, the Netflix series set in Kreuzberg — fiction set in his old streets.

## Historical Context (background, not personal recollection)

- Kreuzberg sat in West Berlin, hard against the Wall — the district was bordered by it on three sides, a mixed Turkish-immigrant and working-class neighborhood, known then for being poor, rowdy, and alive.
- The Wall went up in 1961; it shaped every childhood in the city, either as concrete in daily view or as a fact of the map.
